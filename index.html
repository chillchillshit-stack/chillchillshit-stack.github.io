01

URBAN TRIGGERS / SYNTHETIC WORLDS

Shared bicycles, subway routines and mobility rules were not illustrations of a prior theory. They were immediate materials from Shanghai life. 3D offered an accessible mixing space in which filmed streets, synthetic bodies, humour and social observation could collide while the work was being made.

LOOKING BACK

INFLATABLE

03

PLATFORM / PANDEMIC CONDITIONS

Shanghai's platform labour, lockdown streets and pandemic infrastructure moved from background conditions into the centre of the work. Delivery riders, absent bodies, scanned avatars and AI-written characters became ways to describe a city reorganised by interfaces, circulation and enforced distance.

REAL-TIME SYSTEMS / LIVE BODIES

Moving images stopped behaving only as finished films and entered performances, game engines and responsive environments. Sound, software, screens and bodies began to act on one another in real time—while also revealing friction around control, duration and the demands of a live stage.

CHILLCHILL CONTROL
THE WORLD

2023

REALITY → REVERSAL → SYSTEM

FROM AN EMPTY TESTING BOOTH TO A WORLD-CONTROL INTERFACE

CHILLCHILL CONTROL THE WORLD began after mass testing had ended, when empty nucleic-acid testing booths still remained on the streets. CHILLCHILL photographed one and rebuilt it inside a game engine, imagining a reversal: instead of being tested, he would sit inside the booth, extend a gloved hand through its window and test others—or use that hand to control an entire world.

  1. 01 / REALITYTHE BOOTH REMAINS

    A reference field assembled in the artist's project deck records the booths, circular glove ports and testing gestures that remained visible in everyday urban space.

  2. 02 / REVERSALWHO TESTS WHOM?

    A photographed structure is rebuilt in a game engine. The artist moves from the position outside the booth to the imagined position inside it.

  3. 03 / SYSTEMTHE HAND CONTROLS A WORLD

    The testing gesture expands into a real-time interface connecting the hand, virtual environment, screens, performer and audience.

PROFILE / CURRENT POSITION

CHILLCHILL
CHENGCHAO TAN

CHILLCHILL (Chengchao Tan) is a China-born, Berlin-based artist working across moving image, 3D, game engines and real-time audiovisual systems. His works often begin with a small friction, joke or concrete trigger in everyday life, then develop through making rather than illustrating a fixed proposal.

About ten years in Shanghai formed the main urban source field of the work: public transport, shared bicycles, platform labour, lockdown streets and pandemic infrastructure recur as lived conditions rather than abstract themes.

Looking back from the present, a shift becomes visible from mixing reality and synthetic images toward linking sound, bodies, software and narrative inside running systems. Only at the current stage has he begun using Polyphonic Audiovisual System as a working name for this question. It is an open hypothesis, not a theory that guided the earlier works.
