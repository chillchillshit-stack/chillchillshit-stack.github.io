SELECTED WORKS

01

URBAN FRICTIONS / SYNTHETIC WORLDS

Small incidents in the city—shared bicycles, subway routines, regulation and bodies moving through infrastructure—became starting points. 3D was first used as a practical mixing space: filmed streets, synthetic figures, humour and social observation could collide before any larger theory was named.

02

PLATFORM / PANDEMIC CONDITIONS

Platform labour, lockdown and algorithmic life moved from background conditions into the centre of the work. Delivery riders, absent bodies, scanned avatars and AI-written characters became ways to describe a city reorganised by interfaces, circulation and enforced distance.

03

REAL-TIME SYSTEMS / LIVE BODIES

Moving images stopped behaving only as finished films and entered performances, game engines and responsive environments. Sound, software, screens and bodies began to act on one another in real time—while also revealing friction around control, duration and the demands of a live stage.

TO POLYPHONY

“Polyphony” is a working hypothesis named at the current stage. This path looks back from the present; it does not mean the earlier works were guided by that concept.

What becomes visible now is a changing relation between images, sound, narrative, bodies and software—not a plan that existed from the beginning.

WORKING HYPOTHESIS / NOT YET SETTLED

HUMAN SEQUENCER ONGOING RESEARCH

Collective movement, image systems and the body as a sequencer. This is presented as current material rather than a completed work: its format, relations and public form are still being tested.

Still open: can human movement, image, sound and a running system remain active without one layer becoming the permanent master?
